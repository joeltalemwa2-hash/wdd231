Article 6: Membership

Become a Member or Partner

Membership is open to anyone aged 18 or above who is willing to support our objectives. Organizations and well-wishers are welcome to explore partnership too.

Why Join

What Membership Looks Like

Have a voice. Attend monthly general meetings and help shape our direction.

Stay connected. Get access to our member WhatsApp group and updates.

See the impact. Open access to financial records and program updates.

Grow with us. Volunteer for outreach, mentorship, or skills-based support.

Article 6(b): Members shall conduct themselves with integrity and respect the values of the Organization. Membership may be terminated by the Executive Committee for misconduct or violation of the Constitution.
