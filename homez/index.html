Our Mission

To empower vulnerable families and children through education support, HIV/AIDS awareness, and community development programs.

Headquartered in Masaka City, our work extends to surrounding sub-counties as approved by the Executive Committee — guided always by integrity, transparency, and respect for the communities we serve.

What We Do

Programs Rooted in Real Needs

Every program traces back to one of our five constitutional objectives — built with, not for, the families we serve.

Education Support

School fees, scholastic materials, and mentorship so vulnerable children can stay in the classroom.

HIV/AIDS Awareness

Prevention education, confidential counselling, and ongoing support for affected individuals and families.

Income-Generating Projects

Small enterprise initiatives that build self-reliance and reduce poverty at the household level.
