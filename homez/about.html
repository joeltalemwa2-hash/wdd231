Who We Are

A Community Built on Hope

Homez Community Development Initiative is a grassroots organization headquartered in Masaka City, Uganda — working hand in hand with families, volunteers, and well-wishers to build a future where every household can thrive.

Article 3

Our Vision

A community where every family has access to education, health information, and economic opportunity.

Article 4

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable families and children through education support, HIV/AIDS awareness, and community development programs.

Article 5

Our Objectives

Five commitments guide every program we run and every partnership we form.

01

Identify & Support Vulnerable Families

To identify and support vulnerable children with school fees and scholastic materials.

02

HIV/AIDS Awareness

To promote HIV/AIDS awareness, prevention, counselling, and support to affected individuals and families.

03

Community Outreach

To conduct community outreach and aid vulnerable families.

04

Income-Generating Projects

To initiate small income-generating projects to promote self-reliance and reduce poverty.

05

Partnership

To partner with government bodies, NGOs, and well-wishers to mobilize resources for community development.

Article 2

Rooted in Masaka, Reaching Further

The Organization is headquartered in Masaka City, Uganda. Our operations may extend to surrounding sub-counties as approved by the Executive Committee — always growing in step with the trust and needs of the communities we serve.

Membership is open to any individual aged 18 years or above who is willing to support our objectives. Members are expected to conduct themselves with integrity and respect for the values of the Organization.
