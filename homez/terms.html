2. About the Organization

Homez Community Development Initiative is a community-based organization headquartered in Masaka City, Uganda, governed by a written Constitution and an elected Executive Committee. Our operations may extend to surrounding sub-counties as approved by the Executive Committee.

3. Membership

  • Membership is open to any individual aged 18 years or above who is willing to support the Organization's objectives.
  • Members are expected to conduct themselves with integrity and respect the values of the Organization at all times.
  • Membership may be terminated by the Executive Committee in cases of misconduct or violation of the Constitution.
  • Submitting a membership request through this website does not guarantee acceptance; applications are reviewed by the Executive Committee.

4. Donations & Finances

  • Donations may be made through member contributions, mobile money, bank transfer, in-kind giving, or in person at general meetings.
  • All income and expenditure is recorded by the Treasurer and presented at general meetings, in line with Article 9 of the Constitution.
  • No funds are withdrawn from Organization accounts without the joint approval of the Chairperson and Treasurer.
  • Financial records are open to inspection by any member upon reasonable request.
  • Donations are voluntary contributions to support our community programs and, unless otherwise stated, are non-refundable once received and allocated.

5. Member Conduct & Chat Group

Our member WhatsApp group exists to keep members connected and informed. By joining, you agree to:

  • Treat all members and well-wishers with respect and courtesy.
  • Keep discussion relevant to the Organization's work, meetings, and community matters.
  • Avoid sharing spam, unrelated promotions, or content that violates the Constitution's values of integrity and respect.
  • Understand that the group is administered by appointed members and that conduct violations may result in removal from the group, consistent with Article 6(c) of the Constitution.
