Group Guidelines

How We Keep the Chat Useful for Everyone

A few simple expectations, in line with Article 6(b) of our Constitution.

Be respectful. Treat fellow members and well-wishers with courtesy at all times.

Stay on topic. Keep discussion focused on meetings, programs, and community matters.

No spam. Avoid unrelated promotions, forwards, or chain messages.

Use it to help others. Share updates, ask questions, and offer support to fellow members.
