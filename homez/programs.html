Article 5: Objectives in Action

Programs That Meet Real Needs

Every program below ties directly to our constitutional objectives — built to support vulnerable families and children across Masaka City and its surrounding sub-counties.

School Fees & Scholastic Materials

We identify vulnerable children at risk of dropping out and provide school fees, books, and supplies to keep them in the classroom.

Ongoing

Mentorship for Vulnerable Children

Structured mentoring that supports children's wellbeing alongside their academic progress.

Ongoing

HIV/AIDS Awareness Campaigns

Community sessions on prevention and accurate health information, reducing stigma and spreading life-saving knowledge.

Ongoing

Confidential Counselling & Support

One-on-one and group counselling for individuals and families affected by HIV/AIDS.

Ongoing

Door-to-Door Family Outreach

Volunteers and recruits visit households to identify needs and connect families with the right support.

Ongoing

Aid Distribution for Vulnerable Families

Essential item drives organized around general meetings and community events.

Ongoing

Small Income-Generating Projects

Seed support and training for household-level enterprise, helping families build lasting self-reliance.

Ongoing

Skills & Savings Groups

Practical skills training paired with savings groups that strengthen household income over time.

Ongoing

Government & NGO Partnerships

We partner with government bodies, NGOs, and well-wishers to mobilize resources for community development.

Ongoing
