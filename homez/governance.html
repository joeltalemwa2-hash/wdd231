Our Constitution

How We Govern Ourselves

The full text of our governing constitution, organized by article. Tap any article to expand it.

Art. 1Name

The organization shall be known as Homez Community Development Initiative, hereinafter referred to as "the Organization."

Art. 2Location

The Organization shall be headquartered in Masaka City, Uganda. Operations may extend to surrounding sub-counties as approved by the Executive Committee.

Art. 3Vision

A community where every family has access to education, health information, and economic opportunity.

Art. 4Mission

To empower vulnerable families and children through education support, HIV/AIDS awareness, and community development programs.

Art. 5Objectives

The objectives of the Organization shall be:

  • To identify and support vulnerable children with school fees and scholastic materials.
  • To promote HIV/AIDS awareness, prevention, counselling, and support to affected individuals and families.
  • To conduct community outreach and aid vulnerable families.
  • To initiate small income-generating projects to promote self-reliance and reduce poverty.
  • To partner with government bodies, NGOs, and well-wishers to mobilize resources for community development.
Art. 6Membership

(a) Membership is open to any individual aged 18 years or above who is willing to support the objectives of the Organization.

(b) Members shall conduct themselves with integrity and respect the values of the Organization.

(c) Membership may be terminated by the executive committee for misconduct or violation of the Constitution.

Art. 7Executive Committee

The Organization shall be governed by the following elected officials:

  • Chairperson — Overall leadership and representation
  • Vice Chairperson — Assists the Chairperson and acts in their absence
  • Secretary — Records all meetings, correspondence, and official documents
  • Treasurer — Manages all financial records and funds
  • Mobilizer — Engages the community and recruits members and volunteers

All officials shall be elected by members at a general meeting and shall serve a term of two (2) years.

Art. 8Meetings

(a) The Organization shall hold a general meeting at least once every month.

(b) Emergency meetings may be called by the Chairperson or upon written request by at least three (3) members.

(c) A quorum shall consist of at least half of the registered members.

(d) Minutes of all meetings shall be recorded by the Secretary.

Art. 9Finances

(a) Funds shall be raised through member contributions, donations from well-wishers, grants, and fundraising activities.

(b) All income and expenditure shall be recorded by the Treasurer and presented at each meeting.

(c) No funds shall be withdrawn without the approval of both the Chairperson and Treasurer.

(d) Financial records shall be open to inspection by any member upon reasonable request.

Art. 10Amendments

This Constitution may be amended by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of members present at a duly convened general meeting. Proposed amendments must be communicated to members at least seven (7) days before the meeting.

Art. 11Dissolution

If the Organization is dissolved by a two-thirds majority decision of members, all remaining assets, after settling debts, shall be donated to another registered community-based organization with similar objectives, as agreed by members.

Adopted and signed by the Chairperson and Secretary of the Organization. Full signed copy available for inspection by members upon request, per Article 9(d).
