About MarketHub UG

Connecting Uganda's buyers and sellers since 2019.

Our Mission

MarketHub UG was born in Masaka with a simple belief: Ugandan artisans, farmers, and entrepreneurs deserve a world-class platform to reach buyers everywhere in the country.

Today we connect over 380 verified vendors with 18,000+ happy customers, offering everything from handwoven crafts to specialty coffee — all with the trust and convenience of pay-on-delivery.

Built by TALJOE Studios, Masaka. Proudly Ugandan. 🇺🇬
